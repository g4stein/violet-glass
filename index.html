Design thinking

Violet Glass treats the controls as objects floating over the film: frosted violet glass, soft rim light and a neon magenta timeline. The glass is dark and blurred enough to stay readable over bright footage. A scrim at the bottom of the frame helps too.

The layout follows the player's width. On desktop the timeline runs the full width and play, back and forward sit in the middle of the bar, like most modern players. On tablets and phones those three become large glass orbs over the picture for easy thumb reach, with a compact pill dock below. On phones everything shrinks so the film stays visible, and fullscreen rotates Android phones to landscape. Speed, quality, subtitle language, loop and picture-in-picture live in Settings, and the labels follow your browser's language.

Every control works from the keyboard with a visible focus ring. Touch targets are at least 44px on touch screens, and motion, transparency and contrast follow system preferences.
